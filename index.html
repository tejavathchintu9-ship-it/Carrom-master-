HOW TO PLAY

  • Position: drag the slider (or the striker) to place it on your baseline.
  • Aim & shoot: touch the board, drag backwards, and release. A longer pull means more power.
  • Black & White: you are white, the opponent is black. Pocket all your coins to win. Pocketing your own coin gives another turn.
  • Queen (red): must be covered by pocketing one of your own coins on the same turn, or it returns to the centre. A covered Queen is worth 3 points.
  • Foul: pocketing the striker costs you a turn and returns one of your pocketed coins.
  • Freestyle: every coin scores: white 10, black 20, Queen 50. First to 160 wins. A foul costs 10 points.
  • Timer: you have 25 seconds per shot.
